Build one, then lift it

The guided builder and the logger, end to end. Add blocks, tap Start session, and the thing you just wrote is the thing you log.

New session

Try: Warm-up (90 s cardio + a movement) → Superset with a ladder → a straight lift → Cool-down. Then Start session and log it — and in the superset, pull the partner up to swap which movement leads.

The builder

Four block kinds, each a short loop rather than one long form. Warm-up and cool-down author a list of pieces in one pass — name, seconds or reps, add another — which is the change that makes “90 s cardio of choice” expressible at all.

scheme
Straight stamps identical sets, the way it works today. Ladder prefills reps falling and RPE climbing to a final max. Custom opens the per-set grid. Every scheme lands in the same per-set grid before you commit, so nothing is authored blind.
equipment
Barbell / dumbbell / machine / bodyweight sets the jump size and whether plate maths appears in the logger. Bodyweight skips the weight question entirely.
superset
Two movements authored back to back into one block. The logger renders their rounds interleaved, and the pair is not fixed: grab the partner's grip and pull it up to rotate the order for every round you have not started yet. Rounds already underway keep the order they happened in — that is history, not a preference.

What carries across

Nothing is translated at the hand-off — the builder writes the shapes the engine already stores, and the logger reads them. A planned set stays exactly {t, rpe}; the opener you enter becomes the coaching anchor; the equipment choice becomes the increment the ladder rounds to.

Still mock

No persistence, no exercise catalogue behind the name field, no validation beyond empty names. Edit-a-block reopens as a fresh add. Those are real work in the app and deliberately absent here.
